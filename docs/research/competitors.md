# Research: Competitive landscape

Gathered 3 October 2026. The fact-check section at the end corrects some claims in the
report; where they differ, the fact-check is the version to use.

## Report

# CAC competitive landscape (as of 2026-10-03)

**Verdict.** Across about 50 searches and 50 opened pages I found nobody combining all four pillars: (1) a business-owned local agent, (2) a public/private knowledge graph, (3) generative UI from owner-approved components on the existing site, (4) an agent-facing endpoint. Each slice exists separately, mostly as cloud SaaS or platform-mediated channels. Restaurants are already orderable and bookable inside ChatGPT and Claude, so pitch the combination and the ownership model, not "restaurants are invisible to agents".

## Restaurant and SMB layer
- **[Square](https://squareup.com/us/en/press/claude-chatgpt-integrations)**: ChatGPT app and Claude plugin (Jul 1 2026). US food sellers with Online Ordering are auto-enrolled, no marketplace commission, built on UCP. Gap: the restaurant is a row in Square's catalog; ordering only.
- **[Toast](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/arriving-now-ai-powered-toast-123000618.html)**: agentic ordering inside Google Ask Maps via UCP for Food (Aug 6 2026). Same gap.
- **OpenTable / Resy / Yelp**: bookable [inside ChatGPT](https://www.androidauthority.com/chatgpt-restaurant-reservations-and-waitlists-3696712/) since Aug 10 2026; Resy [in Claude](https://www.verdictfoodservice.com/news/amex-links-resy-with-claude-for-restaurant-bookings/) (Apr 2026); [OpenTable Concierge](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/opentable-launches-gen-ai-powered-concierge-to-arm-diners-with-instant-insights-for-its-60-000-global-restaurants-302504834.html) answers diner questions across 60,000+ restaurants; [Yelp Host](https://blog.yelp.com/news/yelp-host-yelp-receptionist-launch/) from $149/mo. Gap: the aggregator owns ranking and the guest relationship.
- **[Owner.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/owner-raises-240m-led-by-goldman-sachs-alternatives-to-build-the-ai-native-platform-for-every-local-business-302862420.html)**: site, ordering, CRM and AI agents; [$499/mo](https://sacra.com/c/owner/); $240M Series D at $2.3B (Aug 28 2026), over $100M ARR, expanding to salons and grocers. Gap: cloud, replaces the site, no agent endpoint announced.
- **[Popmenu](https://www.restolabs.com/blog/popmenu-pricing)** ($179-$499/mo; AI Answering about $150/mo) and **[Slang.ai](https://www.slang.ai/post/series-b)** ($36M Series B, $68M total, 2,000+ locations): phone and marketing AI.
- **[Marqii MCP](https://blog.marqii.com/introducing-marqii-mcp-and-how-to-set-it-up/)** (Jun 2026): owner-side connector for menus and hours, not guest-facing.
- **[Wix Symphony](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/WIX/wix-launches-symphony-by-wix-a-new-standalone-multi-agent-system-612a7gcfyst5.html)** (Aug 11 2026) and **[GoDaddy Airo.ai](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/godaddy-brings-agentic-ai-to-small-businesses-with-launch-of-airoai-302614911.html)** plus [ANS](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-and-godaddy-partner-to-help-enable-an-open-agentic-web/): cloud back-office agent teams and DNS-based agent identity.

## Agent-ready web infrastructure
- **[NLWeb](https://github.com/nlweb-ai/NLWeb)** (Microsoft, MIT, ~6.3k stars): [one index answers humans at /ask and agents at /mcp](https://presenc.ai/research/nlweb-adoption-2026). Architecturally closest. Gap: no public/private partition, no approved-UI catalog, no owner goals; adopters are still essentially the launch list.
- **[Salespeak](https://salespeak.ai/blog/nlweb-mcp-endpoint-out-of-the-box)**: cloud B2B site concierge plus an NLWeb-compatible MCP endpoint. Closest commercial dual-audience product; cloud, text chat.
- **Cloudflare**: [Markdown for Agents](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/03/cloudflare-crawler/), a [no-code WebMCP toggle](https://blog.cloudflare.com/webmcp/), AI Search and x402 ([Aug 6 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-agentic-internet/)). Edge plumbing without business logic.
- **[Shopify](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/storefront-mcp)**: per-store UCP MCP endpoints for catalog, cart and checkout. Products only, Shopify-hosted.
- **[Scrunch AXP](https://www.sitecore.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/sitecore-acquires-scrunch-to-help-brands-influence-discovery--and-buying-decisions)** (bought by Sitecore, Jun 3 2026; 500+ brands) and **[Profound](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/aeo-startup-profound-hits-unicorn-valuation-raises-180m-series-d-7-months-after-last-round/)** ($180M at $1.8B, Sep 2026): enterprise AI visibility, read-only.
- **[Google Business Agent](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/agentic-commerce-ai-tools-protocol-retailers-platforms/)**: branded chat on Google Search for Merchant Center retailers, not on the owner's site.

## On-site concierge and generative UI
- **[Intercom Fin](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)** ($0.99/outcome), **[Agentforce](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/)** ($2/conversation), **[Alhena](https://alhena.ai/pricing)** ($199-$999/mo), **[Rep AI](https://www.finsmes.com/2026/05/rep-ai-raises-6-2m-in-funding.html)** ($14.4M raised), **[Daydream](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/daydream-launches-ai-powered-search-and-discovery-solution-for-fashion-brands-and-retailers-302837597.html)**, **[Algolia MCP Server](https://www.algolia.com/about/news/algolia-launches-production-grade-mcp-for-agentic-commerce)**: cloud, e-commerce or support, metered.
- **[A2UI v0.9](https://developers.googleblog.com/a2ui-v0-9-generative-ui/)** (Google, Apr 17 2026) standardizes bring-your-own component catalogs over MCP, A2A and AG-UI. **[Thesys OUI-1](https://runtimewire.com/article/thesys-ships-oui-1-local-generative-ui-model)** (Sep 2026) is an open-weight model limited to declared components. The gen-UI layer is a commodity. The spec is called A2UI; I found no "ADUI".

## Local appliances
[NemoClaw's shipped examples](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/run-local-ai-agents-with-faster-models-and-multi-node-clustering-on-nvidia-dgx-spark/) are personal or back-office (news digest, coding, calendar). [Sparki Box](https://www.thexclaw.com/products) ($599, 8 GB) targets internal private AI. I found no local appliance serving a business's public site or external agents.

## Defensible wedge
1. **Ownership.** Platforms make the restaurant a listing in their agent channel. CAC gives the owner the endpoint, the goals and the UI catalog.
2. **One partitioned graph, two audiences.** Humans and agents are answered from the same governed source, covering the long tail (catering quotes, dietary nuance, private events) that food-ordering protocols skip.
3. **Flat cost.** A GB10 is about [$4,061](https://www.storagereview.com/review/dell-pro-max-with-gb10-review) once, roughly eight months of Owner's subscription, with no per-conversation meter.
4. **Measured need.** A [study of 200 Amsterdam restaurants](https://dev.to/blondedevrules/we-built-an-mcp-server-so-any-assistant-can-book-a-table-2le3) found 16 bookable by browser agents and none of 163 working sites exposing a callable interface.

## Strongest objections
- **Distribution.** Square and Toast already route agent orders with no marketplace commission. Why would an assistant call your endpoint? Only [65 of 22,341 surveyed hosts](https://apievangelist.com/2026/07/29/most-published-agent-cards-are-not-actually-a2a/) publish an A2A agent card, 10 of them valid. The consumer integrations I found use [MCP apps](https://workos.com/blog/2026-01-27-mcp-apps), plugins and UCP.
- **Why local?** Menus are public, a restaurant's private data is thin, and a back-office box is an uptime and security liability.
- **Latency.** NVIDIA's [NemoClaw tutorial](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/build-a-secure-always-on-local-ai-agent-with-nvidia-nemoclaw-and-openclaw/) cites 30-90 s per response on the 120B model. Counter: StorageReview measured Llama 3.1 8B FP4 reaching about 2,776 tok/s aggregate under vLLM batching on the GB10.
- **Platform risk.** Cloudflare, Wix, GoDaddy and Google can bundle pieces; Google Search now [builds generative UI itself](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/); Owner has $240M.
- **Standards churn.** A2UI is v0.9, [WebMCP](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/06/webmcp-web-agent-standard-chrome/) is a Chrome 149 origin trial, and [MCP went stateless](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/) in its 2026-07-28 spec.
- **Leakage.** The same NVIDIA tutorial concedes sandboxes do not fully stop prompt injection, so the partition must be structural, not prompt-based.

## Recommendations

- Make Flow 2's primary transport an MCP server (stateless HTTP, 3-5 tools: search_menu, get_hours_or_availability, create_booking_request, request_catering_quote) and demo it live as a Claude custom connector. Publish an A2A agent card at /.well-known/agent-card.json as a thin secondary adapter. Every shipped consumer integration I found (Square, Resy, OpenTable, Yelp, Shopify) uses MCP apps, plugins or UCP; valid A2A cards exist on only 10 of 22,341 surveyed hosts.
- Define the owner-approved component library as an A2UI v0.9-style custom catalog (4-5 components only: menu list, add-to-cart, booking form, catering quote form, hours card). The LLM emits only a component name plus graph node IDs; a deterministic validator rejects any binding to a private node. Call it A2UI in the pitch, not ADUI.
- Enforce the public/private split structurally: separate stores or collections, with the external-agent process holding only a public-store handle. Demo a red-team prompt from Claude asking for customer names that fails by construction. NVIDIA's own docs concede sandboxing does not stop prompt injection, so a prompt-level rule will not survive a judge's question.
- Do not use the 120B model on the visitor path (NVIDIA cites 30-90 s per response). Route typed intents through a small model (8B-35B class) served with vLLM batching, keep clicks deterministic, cache intent-to-component results, and reserve the large model for the background graph-improver sub-agent. Show a live concurrency number (for example 16-64 simulated visitors) to answer the one-box worry.
- Position against Square, Toast, OpenTable and Yelp explicitly: they make the restaurant a row in their agent catalog; CAC gives the owner their own agent, goals and UI. Say CAC complements UCP ordering rather than replacing it, and demo the long tail those protocols skip: catering quote, July 4th hours, a mixed vegetarian/meat party steered toward the owner's goal.
- Include one cost slide using verified numbers: GB10 about $4,061 one-time versus Owner.com $499/month, Intercom Fin $0.99 per outcome, Agentforce $2 per conversation, Alhena $199-$999/month.
- Do not claim that nobody makes restaurants agent-ready; that is false as of July-August 2026. Claim the four-way combination (local, partitioned graph, approved generative UI, agent endpoint) and say you found no one shipping it. Name NLWeb as the closest prior art and state the differences: partition, approved UI catalog, owner goals, local.
- For agent discovery, do not rely on a redirect alone. Advertise the endpoint with an agent card, a link in llms.txt or page head, and serve markdown on Accept: text/markdown, matching the Cloudflare convention agents already use.
- Stretch goal if time allows: return the same approved booking component to Claude as an MCP Apps ui:// resource so the external assistant renders the restaurant's own UI. Ship a structured JSON plus text fallback first.
- Prepare answers for the two hardest judge questions: 'why local when menus are public?' (private CRM and owner goals stay on the box, flat cost, owner sovereignty) and 'how does it scale?' (small-model batching, caching, deterministic clicks, and DGX Spark clustering up to 4 nodes per NVIDIA's June 2026 update).

## Open questions

- Does the consumer Claude app discover or call a site's A2A agent card or MCP endpoint on its own, without the user adding a connector? I found no evidence that it does; search summaries (not primary sources I opened) say Claude and ChatGPT lack native A2A. Test this on-site before building the redirect flow around it.
- A2A v1.0 release date and adoption figures (reported as March 12 2026 and 150+ organizations): the Linux Foundation/HPCwire page returned HTTP 403, so these are unverified. A2A 1.0 itself is confirmed only indirectly via Google's A2UI post and the API Evangelist survey.
- Absence of a four-way competitor is based on about 50 searches; a stealth or very early startup, or another hackathon team, could exist. I could not prove a negative.
- Big Sur AI: the BusinessWire page returned 403. Search snippets say $6.9M seed led by Lightspeed (March 2024) and a PitchBook-listed M&A event in January 2025; current status and pricing are unverified, so I left its numbers out.

- Salespeak pricing ($950-$3,000/month bundles) came from the fetched page summary and may be from site navigation rather than the article; treat as low confidence. Popmenu pricing is from a third-party blog (RestoLabs), not Popmenu.
- BentoBox (now Clover), ChowNow, Squarespace ACP support and Wix Smart Chat plan limits: only search snippets, no primary source opened; I found no agent-endpoint feature for BentoBox or ChowNow.
